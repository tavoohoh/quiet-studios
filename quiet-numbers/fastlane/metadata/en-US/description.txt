Quiet Numbers is a calm, honest number puzzle. No ads. No purchases. No data collected. Just you and the board.

HOW TO PLAY
Pair numbers that are equal (4 and 4) or add up to ten (3 and 7). A pair can sit side by side in any direction, on the same line with only cleared cells between them, or run from the end of one row to the start of the next. Empty a row and it disappears. Out of pairs? Tap + to copy the remaining numbers to the end of the board, up to five times per board. Clear the board to reach the next stage.

A NEW CHALLENGE EVERY DAY
One board a day, the same for everyone. Keep your streak going and look back on the days you've played in the calendar. Missed a day? Replay the last 30 days for practice.

PLAY AT YOUR PACE
• Unlimited hints, for a few points.
• Undo your last move.
• No timers, no energy, no lives.
• Works fully offline.

REWARDS YOU EARN, NEVER BUY
Achievements unlock themes, tile styles, a serif typeface, a row-clear effect, a sound pack and an alternate app icon. Everything is cosmetic: the game is the same for everyone.

GAME CENTER, IF YOU WANT IT
Daily, best-score and streak leaderboards with friends and the world, plus achievements. Your progress follows you across your devices through your own iCloud. Prefer to stay signed out? Everything still works.

RESPECTFUL BY DESIGN
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions.
• No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
• Your games stay on your device.

IPHONE, IPAD AND MAC
Free on iPhone, iPad and Mac, with keyboard shortcuts on Mac. Supports Dark Mode, Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion.
