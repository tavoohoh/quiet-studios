Quiet Solitaire is classic solitaire, calm and honest. No ads. No purchases. No data collected. Just you and the cards.

HOW TO PLAY
Klondike, draw one. Build down in alternating colors, move whole runs at once and put kings on empty columns. Send every card to the four foundations, ace to king, to win. Tap a card to send it to its best place, or drag it where you want. Turn the stock as many times as you like. A short interactive tutorial shows you the ropes.

EVERY DEAL CAN BE WON
Classic deals are checked on your device before you play, so there's always a way to win. If a game can no longer be won, Quiet Solitaire tells you, so you can undo or start fresh.

A NEW DEAL EVERY DAY
The same daily deal for everyone, always winnable. Easy on Monday, expert on Sunday. Keep your streak going, and replay the last 30 days for practice.

PLAY AT YOUR PACE
• Unlimited undo and hints (hints cost a few points).
• Auto-complete when the last cards are face up.
• Win quickly for up to double points. Every win ends in fireworks.
• Works fully offline.

REWARDS YOU EARN, NEVER BUY
Achievements unlock decks (Illustrated, Classic and Pixel), card backs, board themes, a cascade victory and an alternate app icon. Everything is earned by playing.

GAME CENTER, IF YOU WANT IT
Daily, best-score and streak leaderboards with friends and the world, plus 13 achievements. Your progress follows you across your devices through your own iCloud. Prefer to stay signed out? Everything still works.

RESPECTFUL BY DESIGN
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions.
• No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
• Your games stay on your device.

IPHONE, IPAD AND MAC
Free on iPhone, iPad and Mac, with keyboard shortcuts on iPad and Mac (⌘Z to undo, H for a hint, space to draw). Supports Dark Mode and Reduce Motion, with a four-color deck and a large-index deck always available.

Quiet Solitaire is made by Quiet Studios: apps that respect your attention and your privacy.
